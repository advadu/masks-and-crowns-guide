Masks and Crowns

How to play

A race to the magic box for a grown-up and a child aged 4–6. Two screens, one big adventure!

👫 2 players 🎈 Ages 4–6 📱 2 phones or tablets 🌍 English or Hebrew
🗺️

Pick your world

Same game, two magical places. Choose one each time you play.

Superheroes

Hero City

Race over the rooftops of the city at night to the wooden superpower chest.

Hero coinSlime puddleSticky web
Princesses

Princess Garden

Skip through the garden, over the rainbow, to the sparkling jewelry box.

Magic mirrorThorn bushSnowflake
🚀

Get ready in 4 steps

Each player needs their own phone or tablet.

1
Grown-up

Create a new game

Tap “Create a new game” and choose Hero City or Princess Garden. You get a 4-digit code.

2
Child

Join the game

On the second device, join in whichever way is easiest:

📷 Scan the QR code🔗 Tap the invite link🔢 Type the code in “Join a game”
3
Everyone

Choose a character

Pick your hero or princess and type your name. Your name is remembered for next time.

4
Everyone

Roll the dice!

The grown-up taps Start. Both players roll the dice: the higher number goes first. A tie? Roll again!

📖On your very first game, a short story tells you what is waiting in the box.
🃏

Your turn: tap the cards!

Every card is a little surprise. There are three kinds.

Count and hop

1, 2 or 3 lightning bolts (heroes) or magic wands (princesses). Count them out loud and hop that many steps!

✓ Counting practice

Freeze!

A sticky web (heroes) or a snowflake (princesses): you stay where you are this turn. Next turn you play as usual.

Spin for a surprise

The hero coin or the magic mirror! Tap it in the corner of the board and see what happens.

✨

What can a spin do?

A spin opens a hole in the road, or breaks or fixes the bridge.

Holes in the road

A green slime puddle (heroes) or a thorn bush (princesses) pops up on the road.

!Land on it or hop past it, and you go back to the start and lose a heart.
✓The next spin always makes it vanish.

The bridge over the lake

In the middle of the road you jump over the lake, across the steel beam (heroes) or the rainbow (princesses).

!When it is broken nobody can cross: wait at the edge.
✓It is always fixed within 3 rounds.
❤️

3 hearts each

❤️❤️❤️

Falling into a hole costs a heart and sends you back to the start. Lose all three and you rest in your hideout.

Then the other player runs straight to the box and wins the game!
📣

Cheer each other on

💖 Go!

Waiting for your turn? Tap the heart button to send hearts flying across your partner’s screen.

🏆

Who wins?

The first one to reach the box at the end of the road!

Open the treasure!

The superpower chest bursts with masks and stars, the jewelry box with gems, pearls and crowns. Confetti and a fanfare too!

⭐

Great try!

The other player gets a warm, happy screen too. Every game ends with a smile.
