✍️ Building it on Base44

Base44 took care of the parts that usually slow a side project down: sign-in, the database (just two entities, a game room and its players) and hosting. That left my time for the game itself: the board, the characters, the cards and the little surprises.

The most fun part was the two-screen play. Both devices follow one shared game, and the rules run on the server, so when the child draws a card on the tablet, the character hops on the grown-up's phone at the same moment, with the same messages and sounds.

The hardest parts were the small details that matter to a 4-year-old: getting sound to play on iPhones (only after a tap, and even in silent mode), fitting the board on a phone held upright, and timing every animation so the story, the cards and the hops never step on each other. I built it with Claude Code, going screen by screen and fixing what I saw while testing on real phones and tablets.
